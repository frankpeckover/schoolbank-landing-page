What is Myntix?

Simply, a digital ledger for school rewards

Built for behaviour, progress, and a little fun

Built for classroom, wellbeing, and leadership teams, Myntix replaces paper tickets, forgotten tallies, and reward spreadsheets with visible progress, consistent records, and reward choices students can understand.

Recognition

Recognition in the moment

Award positive behaviour credits with clear reasons as moments happen. Staff can reinforce school values while the moment is fresh, so students understand exactly what they did well and why it matters.

Student motivation

Visible Progression

Students can see balances, goals, activity, and reward options without needing to ask. Effort becomes easier to follow between lessons, and recognition keeps its momentum after the bell.

Reward shop

Staff Control

Staff define the reward shop, approve requests, fulfil rewards, and keep expectations clear for every student. Start with one cohort or program, then expand when your school is ready.

Workflow

Simple, fast workflow

Teachers are busy, so Myntix is designed to stay out of the way. Timetables help surface the students in front of each teacher, making it quick to award credits, add a reason, and recognise the moment.

Trust and security

Data reliability and trust

Every credit has a reason, staff member, timestamp, and history. Leaders can review what happened, answer questions with confidence, and protect the integrity of the reward program as it grows.

Our purpose

Better direction, better behaviour

We do not believe in bad students. We believe young people need clear expectations, consistent feedback, and the right moments of encouragement to help them move in a better direction. Myntix exists to make behaviour management easier for staff, clearer for students, and more positive for the whole school community.

Quick answers

What schools usually ask first

Is this real money?

No. Myntix uses an internal school reward currency for behaviour and recognition programs.

Can students spend without approval?

No. Reward requests stay under staff control, so the shop remains manageable and fair.

Can rewards be customised?

Yes. Staff define rewards around your culture, budget, expectations, and age groups.

Can we use houses or year levels?

Yes. Groups are flexible and can be modelled as classes, houses, year levels, programs, or wellbeing initiatives.
