How Myntix works

One clear loop

Staff award credit -> students see progress -> rewards are requested -> history stays recorded.

Recognise

Award credits when positive behaviour happens.

Share

Credits can go to one student, selected students, or a group.

Track

Students see balances, progress, goals, and recent activity.

Request

Students choose from school-defined rewards.

Fulfil

Staff approve, fulfil, refund, or review requests with history intact.

Quick answers

Common questions before a walkthrough

Is this real money?

No. Myntix uses an internal school reward currency for behaviour programs.

Can students spend without approval?

No. Reward requests stay under staff control.

Can rewards be customised?

Yes. Staff define the shop around local culture, budget, and expectations.

Can we use houses or year levels?

Yes. Groups can match classes, houses, year levels, or programs.
